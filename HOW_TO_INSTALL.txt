║           PuzzleVault – Install on Android           ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

OPTION A: Host on Netlify (Free, 2 minutes)
───────────────────────────────────────────
1. Go to https://netlify.com and sign up free
2. Drag & drop the entire "dist" folder onto the Netlify dashboard
3. Netlify gives you a URL like: https://your-app.netlify.app
4. Open that URL in Chrome on your Android phone
5. Chrome will show a banner: "Add PuzzleVault to Home Screen"
   - Or tap the ⋮ menu → "Add to Home screen"
6. Done! It's on your home screen like a real app ✓
   Works offline. Data stays on your phone.

OPTION B: Host on GitHub Pages (Free)
──────────────────────────────────────
1. Create a free GitHub account at github.com
2. Create a new repository called "puzzlevault"
3. Upload all files from the "dist" folder
4. Go to Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch → main
5. Your URL: https://yourusername.github.io/puzzlevault
6. Open in Chrome on Android → Add to Home Screen

OPTION C: Local network (no internet hosting needed)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
If you have Python on your computer:
1. Open terminal in the "dist" folder
2. Run: python3 -m http.server 8080
3. Find your computer's local IP (e.g. 192.168.1.5)
4. On your phone (same WiFi): open http://192.168.1.5:8080
5. Add to Home Screen from Chrome menu
Note: Only works while your computer is on & running the server.

TIPS
────
• Always use Chrome on Android for best PWA support
• After adding to home screen, the app works fully offline
• All your puzzle data is stored on your phone – private & local
• To update the app later, just re-host the new dist folder
